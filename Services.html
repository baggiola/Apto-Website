Online Powerlifting Coaching

Powerlifting coaching is our bread and butter. We work with lifters at every level — from first-time competitors, right through to athletes pushing national and international performances. Whether you want high-contact support or a lower-contact structure with a weekly check-in, you'll get coaching built around your goals, your training history, and what actually drives progress.

High Contact Coaching

For athletes who want continual guidance, fast feedback, and detailed technical support. You'll get WhatsApp feedback with a guaranteed 48-hour turnaround (often sooner), alongside thorough technical analysis and training priorities tailored to your goals. Programming is delivered weekly or by block, depending on what suits you best.

  • WhatsApp feedback (maximum 48-hour turnaround)
  • High-detail technical breakdowns
  • Weekly or block-based programming
  • Rehab guidance from our extensive rehab team
  • Access to a wider group of coaches for troubleshooting and second opinions
  • Competition day handling, where possible

Low Contact Coaching

For athletes who want a lower price point and a clear plan, without needing constant technical input. You'll get a weekly Loom video check-in reviewing your lifting, accessories, and any rehab/prehab work you're running, with space to ask questions throughout the week if needed. Ideal if you're fairly self-sufficient but still want expert eyes on your training.

  • 1x weekly Loom video review (SBD, accessories, and rehab/prehab work)
  • Questions welcome throughout the week (as needed)
  • Rehab guidance from our extensive rehab team
  • Access to a wider group of coaches for troubleshooting and second opinions
  • Competition day handling, where possible

Powerlifting Programming Only

For lifters who want the most affordable entry point and a proven plan to follow, without coaching feedback. You'll get subscription access via MyStrengthBook to a library of powerlifting programmes designed to take you from training blocks into competition prep, with options for different weekly schedules.

  • Subscription access to multiple powerlifting programmes via MyStrengthBook
  • Competition-focused build-ups and meet prep options
  • Multiple training day options (e.g., 3–6 days/week styles)
  • Targeted plans to bring up specific lifts and weak points


Online Hypertrophy Coaching

For anyone who's serious about getting bigger and stronger — building muscle, adding size, and improving body composition so you look better and feel more confident. Our coaching uses an evidence-informed approach, reflecting up-to-date scientific thinking alongside proven systems we've refined through helping people get bigger, stronger, and more consistent in the gym. You'll be supported by a coaching team with strong experience, backed by exercise science and physiotherapy expertise, so your training is progressive, sustainable, and built around the fundamentals that drive long-term results.

  • Hypertrophy-focused training built around the fundamentals
  • Nutritional guidance to support your goal (build, lean down, or recomposition)
  • Support from a wider team including physiotherapists for training continuity and sensible modifications
  • Hybrid option available if you'd prefer some in-person sessions — price and location to be discussed


Nutrition Coaching

A simple, supportive approach to nutrition that fits around real life, whether you are aiming to improve performance, lose weight, or gain muscle. You will get access to our dedicated nutrition coaching app, with check-ins available weekly, fortnightly, or monthly depending on how much support you want. Each check-in is completed through a Google form, followed by a Loom video where your coach reviews your intake and habits, discusses meal timing, provides example meal ideas or meal plans, and sets clear, realistic actions for the next phase.

  • Access to our dedicated nutrition coaching app
  • Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly Loom check-ins
  • Review of food choices, meal timing, and weekly habits
  • Acute weight cutting strategies
  • Example meal plans, meal ideas, and practical food swaps to simplify tracking
  • Guidance tailored to your goal — performance, fat loss, or muscle gain
  • Support between check-ins for questions and troubleshooting (as needed)
