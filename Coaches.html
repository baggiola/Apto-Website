Ilhan Omar

Since embarking on my powerlifting journey in 2019, I have had the privilege to handle a great deal of lifters with varying experience levels, at regional and national competitions.

I firmly believe that effective coaching requires more than just knowledge - it requires communication, collaboration, and a deep understanding of each lifter's unique needs. That said, my passion and curiosity about the science behind movement has driven me to adopt a forward-thinking and open-minded attitude towards powerlifting training.

By developing personalised programs tailored to each lifter's specific needs, I am able to help them consistently carry out their training, to achieve their goals. Therefore, I am committed to working closely with my lifters to build a healthy coach-client relationship and optimise squat, bench, and deadlift efficiency in order to maximize performance.

Jamie Laidlaw

I founded Apto Performance back in 2022 with the goal of creating a platform that helps as many driven individuals as possible reach their health and strength goals. Seeing how it's grown into a team of six coaches supporting over 100 clients has been genuinely rewarding, and it's pushed me to keep raising the standard of what we offer.

I value individualised coaching and rehab, and I've seen first-hand how the right plan and the right support can bridge the gap between good and excellent long-term outcomes. As a competitive powerlifter, I understand the demands and pressures that come with performance sport, but I also coach people at every stage, whether they are experienced lifters or completely new to the gym.

My coaching is evidence-informed, drawing on my BSc (Hons) in Applied Sport and Exercise Science alongside practical coaching experience and what works best for each individual. I also hold an MSc in Physiotherapy, with a keen interest in musculoskeletal injuries, which helps me support clients who want to keep progressing while managing pain, setbacks, or recurring niggles.

Alongside coaching athletes year-round, I had the privilege of coaching for Team GB at the 2026 IPF World Classic Powerlifting Championships, supporting lifters on the biggest stage in the sport. As an athlete myself, I also represented Scotland at the 2024 Commonwealth Powerlifting Championships in South Africa, where I won the -105kg Open class and set the heaviest deadlift in Scottish Powerlifting history.

More than anything, I enjoy working with people who are motivated to improve, whether that means building strength, improving body composition, achieving success in competition, or returning to training with confidence after injury.

Peter Young

Building on my current studies in physiotherapy, I take a holistic approach to coaching, ensuring each athlete's programme is tailored to their individual needs. Drawing on both my own experience and the evidence base, this approach not only helps maximise performance and progress, but also supports long-term enjoyment and longevity in the sport. Over the past two years, this philosophy has allowed me to successfully coach athletes to strong performances at both regional and national level meets.

Alongside coaching, I have been a competitive powerlifter for the past four years. During this time, I've won multiple Scottish titles and achieved podium finishes at both British Junior and Sub-Junior age-grade competitions. I currently hold the record for the highest male GL points in Scotland. However, alongside the successes, I've also experienced setbacks and losses within the sport. This allows me to empathise with and support my athletes through both the highs and the more challenging periods of their training and competitive journey.

Alasdair Wilson

I have been competing in Powerlifting since 2019 and coaching powerlifting since 2021 and since then have had the privilege of coaching a wide range of individuals from complete beginners to international medallists and champions. Alongside this I am a divisional level referee, an active competitor, have served as president and head coach for Stirling University Powerlifting, and have coached internationally for team Scotland.

I value individualised and integrated coaching, rehab with high attention to detail and planning in programming. As someone who has competed internationally, I understand how important the right plan and support is in achieving the best possible outcomes and aim to coach athletes how I would like to be coached. I firmly believe in athlete improvement going beyond the program, and am committed to achieving every client’s goals while also ensuring their continued athletic development.

My educational background in sports science along with my practical sports coaching experience outside of powerlifting has allowed me to develop my broader skillset as a coach and use the appropriate evidence-based tools for the long term development of each individual. I also have a keen interest in musculoskeletal injuries, and my studies in physiotherapy allow me to widen the scope of my coaching to include in depth pain management and rehabilitation.

I feel very fortunate to be in a position to dedicate so much of my time to this sport, and thoroughly enjoy working and developing relationships with every client. I find a great deal of fulfillment in helping people achieve their goals, whether that means competing at a high level, improving body composition, or returning to training post injury.
